Job Title: Senior Backend AI Engineer
Department: Engineering / AI Solutions
Location: Remote / Hybrid
Experience Required: 4-7 Years

Company Overview:
We are building state-of-the-art enterprise AI workflows, LLM agents, and automated knowledge pipelines. We are seeking a Senior Backend AI Engineer to lead development of agentic architectures, microservices, and RAG pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy agentic AI workflows using LangChain, LangGraph, and tool-calling frameworks.
- Implement robust RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architectures with vector databases (Chroma, FAISS, Pinecone) and semantic search.
- Develop high-performance backend microservices with Python, FastAPI, and asynchronous message queues.
- Integrate Model Context Protocol (MCP) and third-party AI APIs.
- Build automated testing suites, CI/CD pipelines, and monitor LLM latency, cost, and reliability.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers to deliver intuitive AI-driven user experiences.

Requirements & Qualifications:
- 4+ years of professional backend software engineering with Python (FastAPI, Flask, or Django).
- Proven hands-on experience developing LLM applications with LangChain or LangGraph.
- Deep understanding of Vector DBs (Chroma, FAISS, Qdrant) and chunking/embedding strategies.
- Experience with Docker, Git, RESTful APIs, and cloud deployments (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical discipline.
- Strong problem-solving skills, code quality emphasis, and excellent communication.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and clients.
- Experience with local LLM runtimes (Ollama, vLLM) and quantization.
- Familiarity with Kubernetes, Redis, and observability platforms (LangSmith, OpenTelemetry).
